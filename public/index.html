Transparent Privacy & Data Retention Notice

  • Data Collected: Full name, email address, hashed password, and the ticket inventory, external listing URLs, sales, and payout records you manually enter.
  • What We Never Collect: TicketPilot never collects or stores your StubHub, Viagogo, or Ticketmaster login passwords or payment card numbers.
  • Isolation & Storage: Every record is scoped to your authenticated user ID via PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (auth.uid() = user_id).
  • Deletion & Backups Disclosure: Deleting your account immediately purges all active database records. On cloud PostgreSQL plans with automated point-in-time recovery backups, encrypted backup snapshots expire according to the provider's retention period (7 days on Supabase Pro; 0 automated backups on Supabase Free).

Reauthenticated Account Deletion

To permanently delete your active account and all associated tickets, listings, sales, and payouts, re-enter your password and type DELETE MY ACCOUNT.
